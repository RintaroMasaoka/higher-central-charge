# Instruction-contract coherence review

Decision perspective: instruction-contract coherence only.

Verdict: PASS within the inspected instruction unit. The authoring and subject-review responsibilities consistently distinguish reader understanding from the availability of a calculation, preserve a bounded optional-support route, and retain independent frozen judgments. This is an instruction verdict, not acceptance of any manuscript.

## Evidence and determination

- **Required depth and baseline.** `authoring.md:13` requires establishing "the reader's starting knowledge" and "the concrete operation or relation the note should teach." At `authoring.md:15`, the author must decide among "meaning and role, application, mechanism, or proof" and "Record the supported reader baseline and the actual support for that depth." `reader-review.md:13` assigns the same depth distinction to the subject lane and requires its supported baseline or already-read support. The instructions do not allow a displayed formula or named result to establish reader familiarity by itself.
- **Visible premises.** `authoring.md:15` requires "the necessary meaning, inputs and next-step warrant visible." `reader-review.md:13` requires reporting missing support "even when the next algebraic step is possible." `reader-review.md:15` makes the premise-demanding word/symbol/relation and its baseline/prior/current witness part of the frozen evidence. These duties cover the difference between substitution and an intelligible connection.
- **Optional explanatory route and inspection.** `authoring.md:15` permits a nearby disclosure or a precise learning reference and requires checking an opened disclosure as its own path. `reader-review.md:13` requires inspecting a deferred entry separately before certifying adequacy, freezing the closed-path verdict first, and applying the same one-paragraph discipline to the selected opened path. Under `reader-review.md:9`, the reviewer executes only its packet; the coordinator must supply the separate entry rather than the reviewer retrieving hidden material. A route's existence is therefore insufficient for certification. Uninspected support cannot receive a certified-adequate verdict.
- **Proportionality.** `authoring.md:15` explicitly rejects requiring a disclosure for established baseline knowledge or expanding every imported theorem into a proof. `reader-review.md:13` repeats the corresponding reviewer constraint: "Do not demand optional proof when meaning and application suffice." The conceptual-adequacy duty is scoped to the learning route; it does not impose universal proof depth or repeated explanation of established premises.
- **One perspective and freezing.** `reader-review.md:3` gives each lane one perspective and prevents either from signing the other. Conceptual adequacy remains within the subject/prerequisite lane. `reader-review.md:7,13,15` consistently freeze the closed visible judgment before any next or opened unit. `reader-review.md:17` leaves `UNKNOWN` and invalid release order unverified. An opened-path judgment cannot retrospectively turn an insufficient closed path into a passing one.

## Limits

The independent reviewer receives no desired reader exit (`reader-review.md:7`). Consequently, its depth judgment can be grounded only in the supplied baseline, permitted governing guidelines and acquired visible route. An author-side learning objective that has no available witness cannot support an independent depth certification; that particular adequacy question remains `UNKNOWN`, rather than becoming a reason to demand a proof. This limits what the gate can certify without contradicting its isolation contract.

No located instruction contradiction was found in this unit. No manuscript, review history, author rationale, source science, wording quality, rendered artifact or build was inspected. This review does not establish that a coordinator supplies a supported baseline, releases optional entries correctly, or retains the required execution evidence in an actual run.
